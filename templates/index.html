Game log

    How to play

    • Two decks with jokers, 4 players. Everyone is dealt 12 cards.
    • Your turn: draw a card, lay down or add cards if you can, then throw one card away.
    • Run: 3+ cards of one suit in a row (A-2-3 and Q-K-A are fine, K-A-2 is not).
      Set: 3 or 4 of the same number in different suits.
    • Jokers are wild and can stand in for any card.
    • Coming out: your first lay-down must add up to 40 points or more, all at once. Number cards are worth their number, J/Q/K are 10, an Ace is 11 (1 in A-2-3), and a joker is worth the card it replaces.
    • Until you've come out you can only draw from the pile. Afterwards you can also take the top discard, but you have to play it in a run or set that same turn. You can also add cards to any meld on the table.
    • Every turn ends by throwing a card away, so always keep one card back.
    • When the pile runs out, the discards are shuffled into a new pile.
    • You win by throwing away your last card.
